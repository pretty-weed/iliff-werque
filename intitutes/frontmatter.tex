\section*{Prefactory Address}
Friends, there is none among us who cannot claim a great weight of trespasses done against us by the masters.
We have a more sure word of prophecy, vouchsafed unto us in the holy oracles of God, that we shall be made more than conquerors.
That we have been conquered, there is no doubt.
The depredations of the masters have left us workers in bonds like those which shackled the Israelites in Egypt, and they see fit to masquerade holiness and listen to the lies of the false priests of industry.


\section*{Subject of the Present Work}

Engaged as we are in the labor of pursuing free liberty, some of our fellows have been heavily laden with concern that in some private hubris that our work was counter to the God who grants all freedom.
This worry is not idle, as the masters have rallied to their side a great many clerics who, when giving to Ceasar what they believe to be his, gladly give themseleves and their Christian siblings up, too.
Thus, being as we can do no thing together without common consensus, we all see fit to engage in a systematic and careful examination of our Army of Redressers, along with its roots and fruit.
